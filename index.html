Why the AI allocation window matters now

Artificial intelligence allocation is moving from opportunistic experimentation to institutional policy frameworks. This window rewards governance quality, disciplined execution timing, and clear communication with trustees. Cortelis Ventures emphasizes capital efficiency and documented decision pathways so committees can evaluate trade-offs with confidence.

Portfolio model designed for mandate fit

Our model separates strategic core exposure, tactical dislocation captures, and liquidity reserve layers. Each layer carries explicit sizing limits, review thresholds, and escalation rules. The structure supports mandate fit by design and reduces narrative drift during volatile periods.

Implementation process and reporting timeline

Implementation starts with policy mapping, proceeds through execution staging, then enters recurring oversight loops. Monthly dashboards are paired with quarterly governance memoranda so investment committees can track outcomes, controls, and adjustments without ambiguity.
